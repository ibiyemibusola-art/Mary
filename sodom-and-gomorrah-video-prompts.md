# Sodom and Gomorrah: Video Prompts (10-second clips)

There are 58 clips of about 10 seconds each, roughly 9½ minutes in total, matching `sodom-and-gomorrah-tts.txt`.
**VO** is the narration line the clip sits under. Each clip has one prompt and one camera move.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Cinematic ancient African biblical epic, photorealistic, dark-skinned African characters, wrapped earth-tone cloth, beads, cowrie shells, copper jewelry, red earth, acacia and great shade trees, sun-baked clay architecture, warm golden light, rich shadows, film grain, 35mm anamorphic, epic and reverent mood, 16:9, no text, no subtitles

## CHARACTER SHEET (keep these descriptions identical in every clip)

- **ABRAM / ABRAHAM:** tall dark-skinned African elder, about 75–99, long white beard, deep wise eyes, indigo-and-ochre wrapped robe, wooden shepherd's staff
- **SARAI / SARAH:** graceful dark-skinned African elder woman, silver hair wrapped in a cream headwrap, beaded necklace
- **LOT (young):** dark-skinned African man in his 20s, short beard, brown wrapped robe
- **LOT (later):** same man in his 50s, greying beard, rich red-and-gold robe, gold armbands
- **LOT'S WIFE:** elegant dark-skinned African woman in her 40s, crimson wrapper, heavy gold earrings and bangles
- **LOT'S DAUGHTERS:** two dark-skinned African young women aged 17–20, simple white wrappers, braided hair
- **ANGELS / THE LORD'S VISITORS:** three tall dark-skinned African men in plain flowing white robes, calm faces, a faint glow on their skin, never smiling or angry
