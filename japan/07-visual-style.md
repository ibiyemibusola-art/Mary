# Visual Style Profile — "Fortune Map 2.5D"

## Art Style
A 2.5D animated map explainer: photoreal shaded-relief terrain underneath, with glossy 3D cartoon props and flat UI graphics on top. Two map modes: Physical (satellite terrain) for geography, resources and the present day; Vintage (aged sepia parchment atlas) for history, empire and comparisons. Props are toy-like 3D miniatures with a clay or vinyl finish. Data appears as huge 3D extruded glossy yellow-gold numbers. Labels are bold white uppercase sans-serif with a soft drop shadow; locations get red teardrop pins.

## Recurring UI Layer
Top-left: four dark circular category badges (hammer, map pin, lightning bolt, wrench). Top-right: mystery and comparison badges. Bottom-left: a segment number box next to a yellow label tag with heavy black uppercase text. Bottom-right: a small gold globe-coin watermark. Scorecard frames: cream parchment cards with yellow headers for the winner side and red headers for the loser side.

## Color Palette
Physical mode: cerulean and turquoise oceans, vivid green lowlands, ochre and tan highlands, rust-brown ridges, white clouds. Vintage mode: taupe and khaki seas, chocolate-brown land, cream paper. Accents: signal yellow (#FFD400), glowing red-orange (#FF4A1C), white, charcoal, gold.

## Lighting Style
Soft, even top-down daylight with a northwest key light; soft ambient occlusion and short drop shadows on props; gentle bloom on glowing elements; a light vignette.

## Camera Style
Mostly top-down orthographic, with an occasional 10–25° oblique tilt, at continental to regional scale. Slow push-ins, lateral pans along routes, and zooms from global view into a country. UI kept to the corners.

## Composition
The subject sits center or center-right; corners are reserved for UI; dotted glowing route lines show flow and trade; one big data number sits over the subject; clouds frame the scene; comparisons use split framing.

## Detail Level
High-detail terrain; low-to-medium detail props and icons; minimal text of one or two words per label.

## Mood
Playful, smart and confident: bright and optimistic for geography beats, muted and serious for history and conflict beats.

## Style Anchor (used in every prompt)
2.5D animated map explainer in the Fortune Map 2.5D style: photoreal shaded-relief terrain map with crisp hillshade, overlaid with glossy toy-like 3D cartoon props (vinyl/clay finish, soft ambient occlusion, short drop shadows), bold white uppercase sans-serif map labels with soft shadow, signal-yellow (#FFD400) and glowing red-orange (#FF4A1C) highlight accents, puffy 3D clouds, gentle bloom on glowing elements, light vignette, clean uncluttered composition with the four corners kept clear for UI, 16:9.
