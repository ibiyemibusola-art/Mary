## Channel Description — Variant A (The Price of Place)
Why does one city boom while another, 200 miles away, goes bust? Usually it's the ground it stands on.

The Price of Place shows how location decides fortune: why a single harbor can outearn a continent, why deserts sink empires, and why a border drawn in a hurry can still cost a country billions today.

Every video starts with one real puzzle and solves it with animated maps, hard data and documented history: rivers, ports, pipelines, tariffs, cartels and the choices that built or broke nations.

No jargon. No lectures. Just what the land is worth.

New videos and Shorts every week.

## Channel Description — Variant B (Wealth of Borders)
Same continent. Same climate. One country rich, the other broke. So what changed? Look at where the line was drawn.

Wealth of Borders explains the economics hiding in geography: why some nations strike gold, why others stall for centuries, and why a single canal or mountain pass can move the fortunes of millions.

Each episode follows one question to its root, using animated maps, real numbers and verified history.

No dull charts. No filler. Just the border behind the money.

New videos and Shorts weekly.

## Logo Generation Prompt
Flat vector YouTube profile logo. A thick glossy gold coin, front-facing, with a warm amber-to-orange radial gradient and a bright specular highlight at the upper left. The coin face shows a simplified world map in heavy black cartoon outlines, slightly tilted like a globe, with two curved latitude lines. A raised inner rim runs around the edge, and a small notched border looks like minted coin edging. A thick white outer stroke surrounds a thin black outline. The background is a dark navy circle with a faint satellite-Earth texture. Bold, clean, high-contrast, readable at 48px, no text, centered, square 1:1.
