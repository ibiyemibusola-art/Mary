## Thumbnail 1 — "Nothing to Dig"
- **Visual Concept:** Japan flood-filled in glowing red on a dark, desaturated relief map of East Asia, with three crossed-out badges labeled "NO OIL", "NO LAND" and "NO MINERALS".
- **Text Overlay:** $4 TRILLION
- **Emotion Trigger:** Contradiction. A huge number sits next to a country that seems to have nothing.
- **Prompt:** YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720. A dark, desaturated satellite shaded-relief map of East Asia and the western Pacific in charcoal and slate tones. The Japanese archipelago is flood-filled in saturated glowing red (#FF2A1C) with a soft outer glow and a thin white outline, centered right. Three small round white badges with black icons sit around the islands: an oil derrick, a land plot and a pickaxe. Each has a thick red diagonal slash and a tiny white uppercase label: "NO OIL", "NO LAND", "NO MINERALS". Top-left: a solid black rectangle with heavy white condensed uppercase text "$4" on one line and "TRILLION" on the next, tightly kerned. High contrast, clean, minimal, flat graphic overlay on realistic terrain, no other text.
