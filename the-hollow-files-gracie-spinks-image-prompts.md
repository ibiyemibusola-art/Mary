# The Hollow Files — Episode 1: Gracie Spinks — Image Prompts

## How to use
- Add the **STYLE BLOCK** to the end of every reconstruction prompt.
- Add the **EVIDENCE BLOCK** to the end of every evidence-table prompt (marked `[EV]`).
- `[ARCHIVE]` slots are real photos or footage. Source them under licence; don't generate them.
- `[CARD]` slots are chapter cards. Build them in your editor, not with AI.
- Each image is held 4–8s with a slow push-in or pan. About 70 generated stills plus archive and cards fill roughly 30 minutes. Re-use a still with a different crop or move when a beat runs long.
- **Never generate Gracie, Sellers, or any identifiable real person.** Figures are faceless: seen from behind, in silhouette, or out of focus.

**STYLE BLOCK**
```
cinematic documentary still, 16:9, photorealistic, 35mm film look, cool charcoal and ash colour grade, lifted blacks, muted desaturated palette, fine film grain, soft vignette, low-key natural light, quiet ominous mood, single small oxblood red accent, no text, no logos, no visible faces
```

**EVIDENCE BLOCK**
```
top-down evidence photograph on a dark brushed steel table, small numbered yellow-black evidence marker, hard overhead light with soft falloff, cool grey grade, fine grain, forensic documentation style, 16:9, photorealistic, no text other than the marker number
```

**Negative prompt (if supported)**
```
faces, gore, blood, injuries, weapons in hands, text, watermark, cartoon, oversaturated, bright daylight, smiling people
```

## Recurring motifs (keep consistent)
- **The gate:** same wooden gate and chain latch in #22, #59, #68 and #93. Generate one, then use image-to-image or a fixed seed for the rest.
- **The rucksack:** same dark-green rucksack in #2, #4, #6, #52 and #72.
- **The note:** same paper and handwriting in #5 and #91.
- **Oxblood:** exactly one red element per frame. Never blood.
