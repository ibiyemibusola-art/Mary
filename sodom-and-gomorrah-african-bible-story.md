# THE VALLEY OF FIRE
## Sodom and Gomorrah: An Ancient African Bible Story

**Runtime:** about 9 minutes at a steady storytelling pace (around 1,450 spoken words, leaving room for music and pauses under 10 minutes)
**Look and sound:** Ancient African look throughout. Characters are dark-skinned, in wrapped cloth, beads, cowrie shells and copper. You see red earth, acacia and great shade trees, long-horned cattle, calabashes, and walls of sun-baked clay. Talking drums, kora and a griot (storyteller) narrator.

---

### COLD OPEN

*[Talking drums. Wind over dry grass.]*

**NARRATOR (griot):** There was a valley that looked like Eden. In its heart stood a city that heaven could not stop listening to. In that city mercy was a crime and evil had become the law.

**ABRAHAM:** Will you destroy the just together with the wicked?

**THE LORD:** Even for ten, I will spare it.

**ANGEL:** Do not turn to look back!

*[Screaming. Laughter. Silence.]*

**NARRATOR:** Come close to the fire, children. This is what the elders tell.

---

### PART 1: THE CALL

**NARRATOR:** After the great flood, the bow in the clouds sealed God's covenant: never again would the waters destroy the world. But heaven had made no promise about fire.

Noah's children's children multiplied and forgot. Then the voice spoke to a herdsman of seventy-five years. His name was Abram.

**THE LORD:** Leave your land, your kin and your father's house, and go to the land I will show you. I will make of you a great nation. Through you every family of the earth will be blessed.

**ABRAHAM:** *(kneeling on the red earth)* Here I am, Lord. Wherever you lead, I will go.

**NARRATOR:** He took his wife Sarai, his herds, and young Lot, son of his dead brother Haran, whom he raised as his own.

---

### PART 2: THE CHOICE

**NARRATOR:** In Canaan, Abram's cattle covered the hills like low clouds, and Lot grew rich beside him.

**LOT:** Uncle, when we left Haran, all we owned fit on twenty camels. Look at what your God has given you.

**ABRAHAM:** Our God, Lot. And He did not give it to me. He gave it *through* me.

**NARRATOR:** But the land grew too small for so much blessing, and their herdsmen fought over the wells. So Abram called Lot.

**ABRAHAM:** Let there be no quarrel between you and me, for we are brothers. The whole land is before you. If you go left, I will go right. If you go right, I will go left.

**LOT:** You give *me* the first choice? It belongs to the elder!

**ABRAHAM:** The promise is not in the land, my son. Choose.

**NARRATOR:** Lot looked at the plain of the Jordan, green like Eden, rich like Egypt along the Nile, watered all the way to little Zoar.

**LOT:** I choose the whole plain.

**LOT'S WIFE:** Cities with walls! I am tired of shaking sand out of my hair.

**ABRAHAM:** My road goes up, Lot. Yours goes down. A tent can move, but the altar you must raise wherever you are.

**SARAI:** You raised him. He will know what is right.

**ABRAHAM:** He will know it. I do not know whether he will choose it.

**NARRATOR:** Season after season Lot moved his tents closer, until he pitched them near Sodom.

*[Low drum.]*

**NARRATOR:** Now the men of Sodom were wicked, great sinners before the Lord.

---

### PART 3: THE CITY WHERE MERCY WAS A CRIME

**NARRATOR:** Sodom shone like a jewel, rich with gold, ivory and spices. By then heaven had renamed Lot's uncle *Abraham*, father of multitudes, and the city honoured his nephew.

But one market morning an old stranger begged for bread, and the crowd laughed.

**NEIGHBOUR:** *(hand on Lot's shoulder)* In Sodom each man eats what is his own. It is the law.

**LOT:** What law punishes mercy?

**NEIGHBOUR:** The law that made us rich.

**NARRATOR:** Lot saw a man whipped for feeding the hungry twice. The third time meant fire. Lot stayed silent, and rose to sit among the elders at the city gate.

**FARMER:** I owed him thirty shekels of silver, and he took my field worth three hundred!

**NARRATOR:** Lot watched a leather pouch slide into the chief judge's hand.

**JUDGE:** The case is closed.

**LOT:** The debt was thirty...

**JUDGE:** Say it again, Hebrew, and the next case judged here will be yours.

**NARRATOR:** Lot did not. At his table, his daughters' betrothed mocked him.

**SON-IN-LAW:** If your God wanted to punish Sodom, old man, He would have come long ago!

*[Laughter.]*

**NARRATOR:** Lot's wife laughed with them. Lot did not.

Every cry, every whip, every door shut on a stranger rose like smoke, until the outcry of Sodom reached heaven.

---

### PART 4: THREE VISITORS AT MAMRE

**NARRATOR:** In the heat of the day, by the great trees of Mamre near Hebron, three men stood before Abraham. He ran and bowed to the ground.

**ABRAHAM:** My lords, do not pass your servant by. Rest under this tree. Sarah, quickly, three measures of fine flour!

**NARRATOR:** He served his finest calf with curds and milk.

**VISITOR:** Where is Sarah, your wife? When this season comes again, Sarah shall have a son.

**NARRATOR:** Behind the hut wall, old Sarah laughed silently to herself.

**THE LORD:** Why did Sarah laugh? Is anything too hard for the Lord?

**SARAH:** I did not laugh.

**THE LORD:** No. You did laugh.

**NARRATOR:** Then two of the men turned toward Sodom. The third stayed behind, and Abraham knew it was the Lord.

**THE LORD:** Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do? The cry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and their sin is very heavy. I will go down and see.

---

### PART 5: FROM FIFTY TO TEN

*[Single drum, slow heartbeat.]*

**ABRAHAM:** *(trembling)* Will you destroy the just together with the wicked? What if there are fifty righteous? Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?

**THE LORD:** For fifty, I will spare the whole place.

**ABRAHAM:** I am but dust and ashes... What if there are forty-five?

**THE LORD:** I will not destroy it.

**ABRAHAM:** Forty? ... Thirty? ... Twenty?

**THE LORD:** For their sake, I will not destroy it.

**NARRATOR:** Then he remembered one more report: *"Your nephew sat at the gate. He saw the injustice. He said nothing."*

**ABRAHAM:** Let not the Lord be angry. I will speak just once more. What if only ten are found?

**THE LORD:** Even for ten, I will spare it.

**NARRATOR:** Then the hill was empty.

**ABRAHAM:** *(whispering)* Let them be there, Lord. Let them be there.

---

### PART 6: THE NIGHT AT LOT'S DOOR

**NARRATOR:** At dusk the two angels reached Sodom. Lot rose at the gate and bowed.

**LOT:** My lords, come to my house. You do not know this city at night!

**NARRATOR:** He baked them unleavened bread. Then the whole city, young and old, surrounded the house with torches.

**MOB:** Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us, that we may know them!

**NARRATOR:** Lot went out alone and, in desperation, spoke the trader's language Sodom had taught him.

**LOT:** My brothers, do not do this wicked thing! I have two daughters... only do nothing to these men, for they came under the shelter of my roof!

**MOB:** This foreigner makes himself our judge! We will deal worse with you than with them!

**NARRATOR:** The crowd surged forward. Then the angels pulled Lot inside, and a blinding light struck every man at the door.

**ANGEL:** Who else do you have here? Sons-in-law, sons, daughters? Get them out! The Lord has sent us to destroy this place.

**NARRATOR:** Lot ran to his future sons-in-law.

**SON-IN-LAW:** *(laughing)* Go back to sleep, old man.

---

### PART 7: DO NOT LOOK BACK

**NARRATOR:** At dawn Lot lingered among his silver. So the angels seized him, his wife and two daughters by the hand, for the Lord was merciful, and led them out.

**ANGEL:** Escape for your life! Do not look back, and do not stop anywhere in the plain. Flee to the mountains!

**LOT:** I cannot reach the mountains. Let me flee to that little town. Is it not small?

**ANGEL:** Very well. Hurry! I can do nothing until you arrive there.

**NARRATOR:** They ran. Lot's wife ran last.

**LOT'S WIFE:** Lot! The gold, the girls' dowry, everything we are is back there!

**LOT:** Everything we are is running, woman!

**NARRATOR:** The sun had risen when Lot entered Zoar. Then the Lord rained brimstone and fire from heaven on Sodom and Gomorrah, on the whole plain and everything that grew there.

*[Thunder. Roaring flames.]*

**DAUGHTER:** Father! Where is Mother?

**NARRATOR:** Lot's wife had looked back. She became a pillar of salt.

**LOT:** Do not look, daughter. Do not look.

**NARRATOR:** Next morning Abraham climbed his hill. Smoke rose like smoke from a furnace. There had not been ten. But God remembered Abraham, and brought Lot out.

---

### PART 8: THE ASHES AND THE PROMISE

**NARRATOR:** Afraid to stay in Zoar, Lot went up to a mountain cave with his two daughters.

**ELDER DAUGHTER:** Our father is old, and there is no man left in the land. Let us give him wine, so our family line does not end.

**NARRATOR:** What happened in that cave, the darkness kept. The elder daughter bore a son, Moab, father of the Moabites. The younger bore Ben-Ammi, father of the Ammonites.

*[Joyful drums, women ululating.]*

**NARRATOR:** And at Mamre, at the appointed time, the Lord visited Sarah. At ninety years old she bore a son.

**SARAH:** God has made me laugh! Everyone who hears will laugh with me!

**NARRATOR:** They named him Isaac, "he laughs." Abraham carried him up the hill above the grey valley.

**ABRAHAM:** Down there, my son, I learned that the Judge of all the earth does what is right. And here in my arms, I learn that He keeps what He promises.

**NARRATOR:** Generations later, Ruth the Moabite gleaned grain in Bethlehem and became great-grandmother of David, the shepherd who faced a giant. An Ammonite princess bore a king to David's son Solomon. The line of the wound and the line of the promise ran side by side, until a night in Bethlehem beneath a different star.

The fire consumed everything in that valley, but the word of the Lord still stands. What He promised, He fulfilled.

*[Final drum. Silence.]*

**THE END**
