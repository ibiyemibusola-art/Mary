## Description

```
Alice Ruggles was 24 when she called the police at 12:40 in the morning to report that her ex-boyfriend, a serving soldier, would not leave her alone. He had knocked on her door late at night and left gifts on her bedroom window sill. The call handler asked her, "What do you want to do about it?" Police gave him a written warning. He ignored it. Eleven days later, she was dead.

SUBSCRIBE and turn on notifications so Alice's story reaches someone who has never heard her name.

This video follows Alice's relationship with Lance Corporal Trimaan Dhillon, the stalking that began when she ended it, her two reports to Northumbria Police in October 2016, and the warning notice that was passed to him through his army barracks instead of in person. It then covers the night of 12 October 2016, the murder trial at Newcastle Crown Court, the IOPC investigation, the Domestic Homicide Review, and the Alice Ruggles Trust set up by her parents.
```
